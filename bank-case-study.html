Customer Churn Prediction Project

Overview

A machine learning project focused on predicting customer churn for a bank using Logistic Regression, SVC, and K-Neighbors Classifier models. The goal is to identify customers likely to leave and help the bank reduce churn through proactive strategies.

Business Objective

Identify customers at risk of churn and understand key factors affecting their decision to leave, improving retention through predictive analytics.

Tools & Techniques

  • Python (pandas, numpy, scikit-learn, imblearn)
  • Exploratory Data Analysis and Data Visualization (matplotlib, seaborn)
  • Data preprocessing: Encoding, Scaling, SMOTE for balancing
  • Machine Learning: Logistic Regression, SVC, K-Neighbors Classifier
