Phones Dashboard Project

Overview

This interactive dashboard tracks phone sales performance, analyzing $22.63M in revenue across 144K units sold. It highlights top-selling products (Samsung Galaxy A13 leading at $0.81M), compares pricing trends, and evaluates customer ratings (avg. 3.99 stars). Key insights include sales volume fluctuations, bestseller vs. Amazon Choice performance (+253.75% difference), and promotional pricing impact. Designed for e-commerce optimization, it helps identify high demand products, pricing strategies, and inventory opportunities.

Business Objective

To analyze detailed phone sales data for improving e-commerce strategies by identifying top products, pricing effectiveness, and customer preferences, thus maximizing sales and customer satisfaction.

Tools & Techniques

  • Data Visualization with Python (matplotlib, seaborn)
  • Dashboard Design and Interactive Reporting
  • Sales Data Analysis and Performance Metrics
  • E-commerce Strategy Optimization
