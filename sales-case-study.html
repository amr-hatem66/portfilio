Sales Performance Dashboard

The Purpose of a Branding Project

This interactive dashboard analyzes Maven Toys' retail performance across 50 stores, 35 products, and 29 cities. It tracks revenue trends by location, category, and time, identifying top-performing stores (like Mexico City locations generating over $100K profit) and product categories (Electronics leading at $5.1M). The dashboard reveals seasonal trends, YoY growth (171.5%), and monthly fluctuations to optimize inventory and resource allocation. Built for decision-makers, it transforms raw sales data into actionable insights for strategic planning.

Business Objective

To monitor and analyze retail sales performance across multiple stores and product categories, providing actionable insights to optimize inventory, marketing, and resource allocation strategies for enhanced profitability.

Tools & Techniques

  • Data Analytics and Visualization
  • Dashboard Creation for Business Insights
  • Sales Trend and Seasonal Analysis
  • Strategic Planning Support
